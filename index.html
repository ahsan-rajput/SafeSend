How it works

Step 1

Encrypt in your browser

Enter text or a file, pick an expiry time, and choose one-time view. Encryption happens on your device.

Step 2

Share the link

The key stays after the # in the link, so it is never sent to our server.

Step 3

Viewed once, then gone

The record is deleted after the first view or at expiry. Later visits show link expired.

SafeSend: self-destructing secure sharing. Your content is encrypted in your browser.
